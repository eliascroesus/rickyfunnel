1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number and country, when you sign up for the free Discord, fill out an application, or book a call, together with a record of your consent to receive emails and text messages (the date, time and page where you gave it).
  • Application answers: for example your goals, your experience with e-commerce, what is holding you back, how much you are able to invest in yourself, and your Instagram handle.
  • Booking details: the date and time of a call you book and anything you write in the booking form.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, text message, Discord or on a call.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage information: the pages you visit, the buttons and links you click, how far you watch our videos, which version of a page you saw, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Where you came from: the website or link that sent you to us (the "referrer") and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source or utm_campaign).
  • Device information: your device type (phone or computer), browser, operating system, the app you opened the link in (for example Instagram or YouTube), your time zone and language. Our servers and hosting provider also receive your IP address when you load the Site; we use it for security and to keep the Site running, and we do not use it to identify you.

Information from others

Our scheduling and messaging tools may tell us when you book, reschedule or cancel a call, open an email, or reply to a message. If you join our Discord server, Discord shares your Discord username with the server.

5. Cookies, local storage and analytics

The Site uses your browser's local storage (a small amount of data saved on your own device) to remember what you typed into a form, which version of a page you saw, and where you came from, for up to 30 days, so a reload does not lose your progress. We count page views, clicks and video plays with our own first-party analytics. We do not currently use advertising pixels or third-party advertising cookies on the Site. Embedded services such as our video player (Vimeo) and booking calendar (LeadConnector) may set their own cookies, as described in their own privacy policies.

You can clear or block local storage and cookies in your browser settings; parts of the Site, like saving your form progress, may then not work. Our analytics do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals, but we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a request to opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (which we do not do).

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you are a lead, community member or client, and afterwards for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, such as keeping records of your consent, resolving disputes and meeting legal, tax and accounting obligations. Site statistics are kept in summary form. When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.
