Earnings Disclaimer

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Ricky's results are not typical, and we do not promise you will make any money.

Most people who try dropshipping or e-commerce make little or no money, and many lose money. We do not have data showing what a typical person who joins our free community or paid program earns, so you should not expect to get results like the ones shown on this Site.

Whose results are shown

The numbers, screenshots and videos on this Site (for example sales totals, orders, views, subscribers and YouTube Play Buttons) are Ricky Shoffner's own results, unless clearly labelled otherwise. They took time, work, testing, money and some luck, and they came after many products and videos that did not work. They are exceptional, not typical, and they are not a promise or prediction of what you will earn.

Sales are not profit

Sales figures shown (for example "$10K in 3 days" or "$11,249 in 4 days") are gross sales (revenue), not profit. They are before costs such as the products themselves, shipping, payment and platform fees, apps and software, advertising, refunds and chargebacks, and taxes. Profit is always lower than sales, and can be zero or negative. Views and subscriber counts do not equal income.

Your results depend on you and on things outside anyone's control

What happens for you depends on your effort, time, skills, experience, budget, the products you choose, the content you make, the platforms you use, competition, supplier and shipping reliability, changes to platform rules and algorithms, and the economy. Many of these are outside your control and ours.

Starting a business has real risk

You may spend money on products, samples, software, ads, tools and education and not earn it back. Online platforms can limit, suspend or close accounts. Only spend money you can afford to lose, and never borrow money you cannot repay to start a business.
